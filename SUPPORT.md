# Support

MamaBot is a prototype. Its content is not a substitute for professional care, and support channels are not monitored for emergencies.

## Get help with the project

1. Read the [root README](README.md) and, for backend questions, [backend/README.md](backend/README.md).
2. Search existing [GitHub issues](https://github.com/PraiseTechzw/mama-mobile/issues).
3. If the problem is reproducible and is not a security issue, [open an issue](https://github.com/PraiseTechzw/mama-mobile/issues/new/choose) with the app version, device/OS, steps to reproduce, and expected versus actual behavior.

Do not post passwords, access tokens, private keys, real phone numbers, patient details, or chat transcripts in an issue. Use synthetic examples and redact logs.

## Health and emergency concerns

Do not use a GitHub issue, the chatbot, SMS, or WhatsApp to request urgent medical care. Contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest health facility. The app cannot assess emergencies.
